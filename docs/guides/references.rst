.. _guide-references:

References
==========

This page collects the main literature used by the qtoolkit guides and by
models implemented in the package.  Individual guides link to the references
that support their equations, conventions, or empirical parameterisations.

SPDC and nonlinear optics
-------------------------

O. Gayer, Z. Sacks, E. Galun, and A. Arie, "Temperature and wavelength
dependent refractive index equations for MgO-doped congruent and
stoichiometric LiNbO3", *Applied Physics B* **91**, 343--348 (2008).
`doi:10.1007/s00340-008-2998-2 <https://doi.org/10.1007/s00340-008-2998-2>`__.

.. _ref-gayer-erratum-2009:

**Gayer et al. (2009), erratum**
